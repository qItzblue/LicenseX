How it works

Your license is created automatically when you download the plugin. Nothing to copy or activate.

1

Download

Get the plugin from the store. Your personal license is built in and never changes, even if you download again.

2

Start your server

The plugin registers the server with your license and prints your personal link in the console.

3

Manage it here

See every server on your license and remove the ones you no longer use to free up a slot.

Questions

The quick answers. Anything else, ask us below.

Where do I find my license key?

In your server console when the plugin starts: it prints your key and a personal link. The key looks like LX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX.

How many servers can I use it on?

The limit is shown on your license above. Each running server uses one slot.

I moved my server. How do I free the old slot?

Enter your key, press Remove next to the old server, then start the new one. The old server stops working the next time it checks in, about a minute later.

Will I get a new key if I download the plugin again?

No. Your license belongs to your account, so every download carries the same key.

Who can see my license?

Only someone who has your key. Treat it like a password and don't post your console log publicly.

My license says blocked or expired.

Contact us using the links below with your key and we'll sort it out.
