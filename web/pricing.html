Questions

The honest answers.

Do I need to change my plugin's code?

No. Upload your finished jar and LicenseX adds the license check around your main class. It works with normal Bukkit, Spigot and Paper plugins.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your existing licenses keep working, so your buyers' servers are never switched off because of a billing problem. You just can't add new plugins or licenses beyond the Free plan until you upgrade again.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Any time, from the Billing page in your dashboard. You keep what you paid for until the end of the period.

How do buyers get their license?

With BuiltByBit, the key is written into the jar when they download. You can also share a direct download link. Either way each buyer has one stable license.

Is it safe to upload my plugin?

Your files are stored privately in your own workspace and are only used to produce your buyers' downloads. The license check is a deterrent against casual sharing; for strong protection also obfuscate your jar.

Which plugins are supported?

Bukkit, Spigot and Paper plugins with a plugin.yml. BungeeCord, Velocity and mods aren't supported yet.
