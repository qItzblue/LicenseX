Questions

The honest answers.

Do I need to change my plugin's code?

No. Upload your finished jar and LicenseX adds the license check around your main class. It works with normal Bukkit, Spigot and Paper plugins.

How does paying on BuiltByBit work?

You buy the plan's resource on BuiltByBit and download its file. It contains an access key: paste it in your dashboard under Billing and the plan switches on.

What about the monthly plan?

Each key adds 31 days. Renew on BuiltByBit and activate the new key; the days stack on top of what is left.

What happens if my plan runs out?

Your existing licenses keep working, so your buyers' servers are never switched off. You just can't add plugins or licenses beyond the Free plan until you renew.

Is it safe to upload my plugin?

Your files are stored privately in your own workspace and are only used to produce your buyers' downloads. The license check stops casual sharing and every copy is traceable; for strong protection also obfuscate your jar.

Which plugins are supported?

Bukkit, Spigot and Paper plugins with a plugin.yml. BungeeCord, Velocity and mods aren't supported yet.
